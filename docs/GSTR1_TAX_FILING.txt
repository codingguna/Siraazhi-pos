1. GST RATE STRUCTURE FOR EDIBLE COLD-PRESSED OILS
   - Groundnut Oil (HSN 1512): 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%)
   - Coconut Oil   (HSN 1513): 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%)
   - Sesame Oil    (HSN 1515): 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%)
   - Inter-state Supplies: 5% IGST

2. B2B vs B2C CLASSIFICATION
   - Invoices with customer GSTIN entered are automatically classified as Table 4A (B2B).
   - Invoices without GSTIN are classified as Table 7 (B2C Others / Retail Consumers).

3. EXPORTING GSTR-1 TO EXCEL FOR YOUR CHARTERED ACCOUNTANT (CA)
   - Step 1: In Siraazhi Pro POS, open the "Reports" tab.
   - Step 2: Choose the filing period (e.g. 01/10/2026 to 31/10/2026).
   - Step 3: Click "Export to Excel (.xlsx)".
   - Step 4: The downloaded spreadsheet contains pre-aggregated sheets:
             1. B2B Invoices (Registered wholesale buyers)
             2. B2CS (Retail walk-in sales state-wise)
             3. HSN Summary (Total quantity & tax per HSN code)
             4. Document Summary (Serial range of issued & cancelled bills)
