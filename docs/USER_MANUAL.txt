================================================================================
  SIRAAZHI PRO POS — STORE USER MANUAL & OPERATIONS GUIDE
  Vanur Farmer Producer Company Limited (VFPC Ltd)
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
   Siraazhi Pro POS is an enterprise point-of-sale, inventory tracking, and GST
   invoicing software engineered specifically for traditional cold-pressed oil
   producers and retail outlets.

2. GETTING STARTED
   - Double-click the "Siraazhi Pro POS" shortcut on your Desktop.
   - The native desktop application window will open automatically.
   - If the window is closed, double-clicking the Desktop shortcut will instantly
     reopen the POS without restarting the server or port conflicts.

3. COUNTER BILLING WORKFLOW
   Step 1: Click any oil SKU card on screen OR scan barcode (or press [F2]).
   Step 2: Adjust quantity if needed using the [+] and [-] buttons.
   Step 3: Toggle GST Mode:
           - Green Toggle ON  = GST Tax Invoice (with CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%)
           - Gray Toggle OFF = Retail Cash Memo (Zero Tax)
   Step 4: Enter customer details (Name, Mobile, optional GSTIN for B2B bills).
   Step 5: Select payment method (Cash, UPI / GPay, Card, Credit).
   Step 6: Click "Print Bill" or press [F10]. The receipt prints immediately.

4. MULTI-PRINTER ENGINE
   - 80mm Thermal: Standard 3-inch roll printer (TVS, Epson, Xprinter, Thermal 80).
   - 58mm Thermal: Compact 2-inch handheld/Bluetooth roll printer.
   - A4 Invoice: Full-page office laser/inkjet printer with legal GST tax breakdown.
   - A5 Slip: Half-page delivery slip.
   - PDF Download: Generates vector PDF directly in your Downloads folder.

5. INVENTORY & PRODUCTION MANAGEMENT
   - Add Production Batch: When fresh oil is crushed, credit the quantity to
     instantly update live stock.
   - Physical Stock Audit: Record stock adjustments with notes (spillage/sample).
   - Low Stock Alerts: SKUs below threshold (e.g. 5 bottles) display warning badges.

6. RAW MATERIAL PROCUREMENT & OPERATING EXPENSES
   - Purchases: Log copra, groundnut pods, sesame seeds, and bottle procurement.
   - Expenses: Record daily mill crushing charges, labour, electricity, and rent.

7. GSTR-1 TAX REPORTS & EXCEL EXPORTS
   - Open Reports tab -> select date range -> click "Export to Excel (.xlsx)".
   - Generates official CA filing sheets with B2B, B2C Large, B2C Small, and
     HSN Summary tables compatible with the GST offline portal.

8. MOBILE STAFF BILLING (WI-FI TERMINALS)
   - Staff on mobile phones or tablets connected to the shop Wi-Fi can open
     the URL shown under Network Settings (e.g. http://192.168.1.5:8080).
   - Staff terminals operate in Restricted Billing Mode:
     Can add items and print bills, but CANNOT view expenses, delete bills,
     or modify product master data.
